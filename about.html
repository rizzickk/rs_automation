About the Founder

Ricardo Ramirez Jr.

I spent five years as a senior analyst inside USAA Bank — one of the largest financial institutions in the country. I designed the reporting infrastructure that replaced fragmented data across four director-led teams. I built automated pipelines that took executive reporting from days to under ten minutes. I built predictive models that caught failures up to six months before they appeared anywhere else. Those systems saved over 5,600 hours a year.

Then I looked at the organizations in my own community — El Paso businesses, nonprofits, healthcare clinics — and saw the same problem I'd spent years solving at the institutional level: fragmented data, reactive reporting, and no clear picture of what was actually working. A construction company that didn't know which jobs were profitable. A nonprofit trying to explain their finances to a board without a reliable report to show them.

These aren't failing organizations. They just haven't had access to someone who builds this for a living. That's the gap I fill.
